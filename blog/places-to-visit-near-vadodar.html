Top 10 Places to Visit Near Vadodara
During Your Stay

When planning a trip to Gujarat, Vadodara (also known as Baroda) is one of the most culturally vibrant and historically rich cities to explore. Known for its royal heritage, temples, palaces, and art, Vadodara attracts travelers from across the country. If you’re looking for the best places to visit in Vadodara or wondering about the top things to do near Vadodara, this guide will help you explore the city and nearby gems. From the grandeur of Laxmi Vilas Palace to the world’s tallest Statue of Unity, here’s a curated list of must-visit attractions that promise an unforgettable experience.

1. Laxmi Vilas Palace – A Royal Marvel

The Laxmi Vilas Palace is not just a landmark but the soul of Vadodara’s royal heritage. Built by Maharaja Sayajirao Gaekwad III in 1890, it is four times the size of Buckingham Palace. The palace is known for its Indo-Saracenic architecture, intricate interiors, and sprawling gardens.

Visitors can tour the palace museum, which houses a rich collection of artwork, weaponry, and royal artifacts. A golf course and the grand Darbar Hall also highlight the palace’s magnificence. For history buffs and architecture lovers, this is undoubtedly one of the top places to visit in Vadodara. Plan to spend at least 2–3 hours here to truly soak in its grandeur.

2. Sayaji Garden (Kamati Baug) – A Family-Friendly Attraction

Spread across 100 acres, Sayaji Garden , locally known as Kamati Baug, is a perfect spot for families and leisure travelers. The garden is home to a zoo, aquarium, planetarium, and a small train ride for kids.

It is not only a recreational hub but also a green escape in the heart of the city. Locals often visit for morning walks, while tourists can enjoy its relaxed vibe. Whether you’re traveling with family or solo, Sayaji Garden offers one of the most enjoyable things to do near Vadodara without venturing too far.

3. Statue of Unity – The World’s Tallest Statue

Located about 90 km from Vadodara, the Statue of Unity is one of Gujarat’s most iconic attractions. Standing at a height of 182 meters, it is dedicated to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, India’s Iron Man.

The site includes a viewing gallery, museum, and exhibition halls narrating Patel’s legacy. The surrounding area also features Valley of Flowers, boating activities, and light-and-sound shows in the evening. If you’re looking for the best places to visit near Vadodara for a one-day trip, the Statue of Unity is a must-see.

4. Ajwa Water Park & Gardens – Perfect for Leisure Travelers

Ajwa Water Park and Gardens is a fun getaway located around 18 km from Vadodara. Famous for its 22-acre landscaped garden and water rides, it’s an ideal destination for families, kids, and groups of friends.

The Ajwa reservoir nearby adds to the beauty of the area. Tourists often combine Ajwa Gardens with Ajwa Water Park for a full day of relaxation and adventure. For travelers who want a mix of natural beauty and entertainment, this is among the most refreshing things to do near Vadodara.

5. EME Temple – A Blend of Faith and Architecture

Also known as Dakshinamurthy Temple, EME Temple is maintained by the Indian Army and is one of the most unique temples in India. Dedicated to Lord Shiva, the temple’s architecture is a blend of contemporary design with influences from all major religions.

The aluminum-covered geodesic structure makes it stand out, and the serene environment offers peace and spirituality. Travelers who want to explore something beyond the typical tourist trail will find this temple both fascinating and calming.

6. Champaner-Pavagadh Archaeological Park – A UNESCO Heritage Site

Just 50 km from Vadodara lies the Champaner-Pavagadh Archaeological Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. This area is home to ancient mosques, Hindu and Jain temples, forts, and stepwells dating back to the 8th–14th centuries.

Pavagadh Hill is also popular for the Kalika Mata Temple, a significant pilgrimage site. This destination perfectly combines history, culture, and natural beauty, making it one of the most rewarding places to visit near Vadodara.

7. Baroda Museum and Picture Gallery – For Art & History Lovers

Located in Sayaji Garden, the Baroda Museum and Picture Gallery is an intellectual delight. It houses a wide range of collections including Indian miniatures, European paintings, Egyptian antiquities, and sculptures.

For art lovers and students of history, this museum is a treasure trove. It also adds educational value to your trip and offers a quiet, contemplative experience away from the bustling streets.

8. Hathni Mata Waterfalls – A Monsoon Paradise

Around 70 km from Vadodara, Hathni Mata Waterfalls is a popular nature spot. Surrounded by lush greenery, the waterfall is at its best during the monsoon season. The natural cave near the waterfall adds charm, and locals often visit here for picnics and short treks.

If you’re a nature enthusiast or photographer, this spot will give you some unforgettable memories. For those looking to escape city life, it is one of the most refreshing things to do near Vadodara.

9. Sursagar Lake – Evening Serenity in Vadodara

Located in the center of Vadodara, Sursagar Lake is a peaceful hangout spot. The large statue of Lord Shiva in the middle of the lake adds spiritual charm.

Visitors can enjoy boating here in the evenings and admire the lake illuminated by lights. Whether you’re on a romantic outing or simply want some quiet time, Sursagar Lake offers a relaxing break within the city.

10. Narmada Canal & Riverfront – A Scenic Escape

The Narmada Canal and Riverfront near Vadodara is a hidden gem. Ideal for a short evening drive or picnic, it’s a great spot for travelers who want to enjoy scenic beauty away from the main tourist trail. The greenery and calm waters make it a perfect stop for couples and families alike.

Conclusion

Vadodara is not just a city; it’s an experience waiting to be explored. From majestic palaces and UNESCO heritage sites to serene lakes and modern attractions, the city has something for everyone. If you’re planning a trip, don’t miss these places to visit in Vadodara and nearby destinations that make your stay truly unforgettable.

FAQs About Places to Visit in Vadodara


1. What is the famous thing of Vadodara?

Vadodara is famous for the Laxmi Vilas Palace, rich royal heritage, art galleries, and cultural festivals.

2. Which place is best for a 1-day trip in Gujarat?

The Statue of Unity and Champaner-Pavagadh are perfect one-day trips from Vadodara.

3. Is Vadodara worth visiting?

Yes, Vadodara is worth visiting for its royal palaces, gardens, temples, and cultural vibe.

4. Does Vadodara have a beach?

No, Vadodara does not have a beach, but it has lakes and riverfronts.

5. How can I spend time in Vadodara?

Explore palaces, museums, gardens, temples, and enjoy local food. Evening walks around Sursagar Lake are also popular.

6. What food is Vadodara famous for?

Vadodara is famous for Gujarati thalis, Kathiyawadi cuisine, sev usal, and snacks like fafda-jalebi.

7. Is Vadodara cheap or expensive?

Vadodara is relatively affordable compared to other big cities in Gujarat.

8. Which hill station is near Vadodara?

Pavagadh Hill and Saputara are popular hill stations near Vadodara.

9. How much time is required to visit Laxmi Vilas Palace?

Around 2–3 hours are recommended to explore the palace and museum properly.
